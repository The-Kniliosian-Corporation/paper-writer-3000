\section{Background or Motivating Example}
Present a concrete case that makes the problem visible to the reader.
Two common patterns work well:\\
  (a) A real scenario where existing methods fail or produce misleading
      output. This is a strong fit for empirical studies.\\
  (b) A small worked example showing what the proposed approach would do
      differently. This is a strong fit for technique papers.

Keep the example short enough to fit on about one page, but with enough
detail that the reader can follow it without reading the full study.
Figures or code listings help a lot here.

If a Background section fits the paper better than a motivating example,
replace this section and define the terms, datasets, or concepts that
appear later. Cite original sources rather than textbook summaries.
