\section{Threats to Validity}
 
\subsection{Internal Validity}
Factors inside the study that could influence the results. Common
examples: tool bugs, author judgment during manual labeling, confounding
variables that were not controlled for. State each threat, then the
mitigation that was applied.
 
\subsection{External Validity}
Limits on how far the results carry over to other settings. Common
examples: programming languages covered, project sample, the time
window of the data, and the choice of benchmarks.
 
\subsection{Construct Validity}
Whether the measurements capture what they claim to capture. Common
examples: proxy metrics, label quality, and the operational definition
of the phenomenon under study.
