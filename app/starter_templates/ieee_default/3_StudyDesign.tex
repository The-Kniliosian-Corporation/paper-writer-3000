\section{Study Design}

For empirical studies, describe what is shared across all research
questions. Put anything specific to a single RQ inside that RQ's
subsection, not here.

Items to cover:\\
  - Subject selection: how the sample was drawn, inclusion and exclusion
    criteria, and a justification for the sample size.\\
  - Dataset: summary statistics in a table. Put the full list in an
    appendix or in the replication package.\\
  - Tooling: scripts, versions, and the link to the replication package.
  - Common analysis steps: statistical tests, effect size measures, and
    significance thresholds used across RQs.\\

For a technique proposal paper, rename this section to ``Approach''. Give
an overview figure of the pipeline, then one subsection per component.
State the inputs, outputs, and assumptions explicitly.
