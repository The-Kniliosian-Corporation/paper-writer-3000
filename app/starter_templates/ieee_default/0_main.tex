\begin{abstract}
Abstract should be written last. 
One paragraph, about 150 to 250 words. Cover these five points in order:
  1. Context and problem (1 to 2 sentences).
  2. What the paper does: study or technique.
  3. Method in one sentence.
  4. Main result with concrete numbers when possible.
  5. Takeaway for the reader.
\end{abstract}
