Draw lessons from the findings for three groups. Each paragraph should
point back to a specific result from Section IV. Avoid generic advice
that could be written without running the study.

Open problems, methodological suggestions, and promising follow-up
studies that this work enables.
 
\subsection{For Practitioners}
Concrete advice for engineers who want to apply the findings in
industrial projects. What should they do differently on Monday morning?
 
\subsection{For Tool Builders}
Features, checks, or warnings that a tool should support based on the
observed patterns. Be specific enough that a tool author could start
coding from this paragraph.
